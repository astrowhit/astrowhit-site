Adventures of the Astrowhit Research Group at AAS245

January 17, 2025 · Kate Whitaker

We had 6 Astrowhit group members (plus several alumni!) in attendance at the 245th meeting of the American Astronomical Society (AAS) in National Harbor, Washington DC from January 12–16, 2025. Our research was featured in two poster presentations and 4 oral presentations, plus a press conference on January 15, 2025. It was a fun week of science and networking.
