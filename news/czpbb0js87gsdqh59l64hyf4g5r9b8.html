Hubble Fellow Sinclaire Manning to join UMass Astronomy

April 5, 2021 · Kate Whitaker

NASA announced the new cohort of 24 Fellows for the prestigious NASA Hubble Fellowship Program, an extremely competitive program that supports postdoctoral scientists in pursuing independent astrophysics research. UMass Astronomy is excited to welcome the soon to be Dr. Sinclaire Manning as a Hubble Fellow into our department in the fall. Sinclaire’s research focuses on studying the enigmatic population of dusty star-forming galaxies in the early Universe. The Whitaker Research Group is so lucky to have Sinclaire joining us!
