Introducing the FCAD Scholars Spotlight

April 7, 2024 · Teaching, Undergraduate Research · Kate Whitaker

FCAD Scholars Spotlight is a novel initiative founded by Moiz Khalil (UMass 24’), under the mentorship of Profs. Kim Ward-Duong and myself. This spotlight provides a unique platform for early-career researchers at all levels – from undergraduates to postdocs – to share their research and contributions to the field of astronomy.
