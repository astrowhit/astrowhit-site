2021 Top 1% Highest-Cited Scientists Announced

November 19, 2021 · Kate Whitaker

Every year, the most influential researchers are identified across a range of 21 fields by the academic analytics company Clarivate on the basis of an exemplary publication record. These scientists are deemed the world’s most influential researchers as the top 1% highest-cited scientists through the last decade. It is an absolute honor to make an appearance on this list (along with other DAWN faculty and UMass faculty) for the first time in my career, no doubt with thanks to the work established through the 3D-HST survey.
