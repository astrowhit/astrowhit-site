NASA Webb’s deepest ever view of Pandora’s Cluster

February 15, 2023 · Astronomy in the News · Kate Whitaker

Take a moment to pause — zoom in — and stare at one of the deepest ever image of our Universe. Coined “Pandora’s Cluster”, the Abell2744 field is a treasure trove containing some of the most distant and lowest mass galaxies ever discovered. The level of detail we can see in these galaxies is unprecedented. This image was publicly released to the community with the UNCOVER survey paper (Bezanson et al. 2023) and shortly after the photometric catalog paper (Weaver et al. 2023)… and now begins the fun part, SCIENCE!
