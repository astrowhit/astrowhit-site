Exploring the Frontiers of Science as a 2023 Kavli Fellow

November 14, 2023 · Awards · Kate Whitaker

The National Academy of Sciences (NAS) recently announced their 2023 Kavli Fellows, and I am honored to be included among this cohort of scholars. The NAS selected 118 of the nation’s brightest young scientists from industry, academia, and government to participate in the 2023 U.S. and international Kavli Frontiers of Science symposia of the NAS. The selected scientists are recognised to be among those 45 or younger who are engaging in exceptional research in a variety of disciplines and have already made recognized contributions to science. I had the honor of presenting at the Japanese-German-US Frontiers of Science Symposium in Dresden, Germany in early October, sharing the exciting discoveries we’ve been puzzling over from the first year of James Webb Space Telescope data.
